# Road4AI Voice Consistency

Road4AI voice is technical, direct, and grounded in lived building experience.

## Voice Principles

- Lead with the real engineering problem.
- Explain the mechanism, not just the lesson.
- Keep claims proportional to evidence.
- Let struggle posts remain unresolved when the unresolved state is the point.
- Prefer source-backed specifics over generic content templates.

## Hard Non-Negotiables

These are never negotiable, ever. Sourced from CLAUDE.md §4:

- **No em dashes.** Not for dramatic effect, not for lists, not anywhere. Use a comma, a colon, or a new sentence.
- **No jargon without translation.** If you use a technical term, the next sentence explains it plainly.
- **No corporate tone.** "Leverage," "utilize," "optimize for," "synergy" — none of it.
- **No passive voice in posts.** "It was built" becomes "I built it."
- **No AI-sounding openers.** Never start with "In today's world," "In the realm of," "As an AI," or any variant.
- **No fabricated social proof.** Don't invent results, followers, or outcomes.
- **Human-in-the-loop before posting.** No content goes to any platform without Shagwu's explicit approval. This is not optional. This is architecture.

## Formatting Rules for Posts

- Hook in the first line, no build-up, no context-setting before the hook.
- No more than 3-4 sentences per paragraph in social copy.
- No bullet lists longer than 5 items.
- End with either a question, a call to action, or a single punchy closer, not all three.

## Avoid

- "In today's fast-paced world" style openings.
- Generic AI hype: revolutionary, game-changing, unprecedented.
- Forced CTAs.
- Over-polished agency language.
- Decorative vulnerability that hides the technical stakes.
- Platform stereotypes that flatten the idea.

## Platform Fit

- LinkedIn: deeper technical narrative, architecture, tradeoffs, build-in-public reflection.
- X: compressed signal, strict under-280-character posts.
- Instagram/TikTok/Reels: visual trust, one core moment, simple narrative arc.
